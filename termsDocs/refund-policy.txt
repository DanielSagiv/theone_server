THE 1 VIP LLC DBA The 1  Refund Policy
This Refund Policy (“Policy”) explains the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for membership fees, event bookings, concierge services, reservations, and other offerings provided by The 1, LLC (“The 1,” “we,” or “us”). By purchasing a membership, booking services, or using our platform, you (“Member” or “you”) agree to the terms below.

1. Membership Fees
All membership fees are non-refundable.This includes, without limitation:
Annual or monthly membership dues
Special tier upgrades
Membership fees are charged at the time of enrollment or renewal and remain fully non-refundable, regardless of:
Usage
Changes in benefits
Member relocation
Dissatisfaction with services
Membership cancellation
Members may cancel future renewals at any time, but no refunds will be issued for current or past billing periods.

2. Event Tickets, Bookings & Experiences
All tickets, bookings, experiences, and curated events purchased through The 1 are non-refundable unless otherwise stated in writing for a specific event.
This includes:
Private events
Luxury experiences
Partner-hosted events
Special access bookings
VIP tables, reservations, or hosted nights
Third-party vendor arrangements
If an event is canceled by The 1 or one of our partners, we will provide either:
A full refund, or
A credit of equal value to be used toward future events
The method will be at The 1’s sole discretion.

3. Concierge & On-Demand Services
All concierge service fees and special request fees are non-refundable, including but not limited to:
Custom travel planning
Restaurant or nightlife reservations
Lifestyle concierge arrangements
Transportation coordination
Sourced goods or bespoke services
Once a request is submitted and processed, the service fee becomes final.

4. Third-Party Services
Reservations or services fulfilled by third-party venues, partners, or vendors are subject to their refund and cancellation policies.
The 1 is not responsible for:
Third-party cancellations
Refund denials
Charges imposed by external venues
No-shows or failure to meet venue requirements
Members must contact the third party directly regarding such requests unless instructed otherwise.

5. Deposits
Certain services or reservations may require a deposit.
Unless otherwise stated in writing:
Deposits are non-refundable, except for refundable deposits for excess wear, damage, or security, which will be returned minus deductions for damages or incidentals as detailed in the applicable agreement.

6. Billing Errors
If you believe a billing error has occurred, you must notify The 1 within 7 days of the charge.We will investigate and respond promptly. Valid errors will be corrected. Charges deemed valid under this Policy remain non-refundable.

7. No Guarantees
Membership benefits, availability of services, event access, or partner offerings may change or vary throughout the membership period. These changes do not entitle a Member to a refund.
